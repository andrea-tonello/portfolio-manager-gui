### Portfolio Value at Risk (VaR)
Maximum expected loss over a specified time horizon in days, given a confidence interval. The higher the number of projected days, the less reliable the results.

The implementation used here is a historical (Monte Carlo) simulation: each scenario replays real stretches of consecutive past days (up to about a month), starting on random days and applied to the current portfolio; longer horizons chain several stretches. Bad days tend to come in clusters, and keeping the days in their real order keeps them together: March 2020's string of falls appears in the scenarios as it happened, not scattered among calm days. Cash is not considered at risk.
