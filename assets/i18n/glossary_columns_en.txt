### Date
Date when the operation was executed

### Account
The account where the operation was performed

### Operation
Type of operation (Buy, Sell, Deposit...)

### Product
Type of product (Stock, ETF, Cash...)

### Ticker
The security's ticker. Follows the Yahoo Finance standard

### Asset name
Full name of the security

### TER
For eligible products, annualized Total Expense Ratio

### Currency
Currency of the operation. Currently supported: EUR, USD

### Exch. Rate
EUR-USD conversion rate of the operation

### Exch. Qty
Quantity of the security exchanged in the current operation. Positive if Buy, negative if Sell

### Price
Unit price in original currency. Negative if Buy, positive if Sell

### Price EUR
Unit price in EUR. Negative if Buy, positive if Sell

### Nominal Value
Direct value of the operation. For securities, equals *Exch. Qty × Price EUR*

### Fees
Commissions applied by the broker for the operation

### Curr. Qty
Current quantity of the security after the current operation

### ABP
Average Buy Price of the security. Prices may or may not include commissions, depending on the asset class and broker. Calculated as *Σ (Qty × Price) / Σ Qty*, over all purchases

### Residual Cost
Residual amount of the asset. Calculated as *ABP × Curr. Qty*

### Eff. Value
Effective amount of the operation net of any commissions

### Cost Released
Cost released after the sale of an asset. Calculated as *ABP × Exch. Qty*

### Gross Cap Gain
Gross capital gain generated by the sale. Calculated as *Eff. Value − Cost Released*

### Cap Loss Gen.
Capital loss generated by the operation. For some products (e.g., ETFs) it can also be generated on purchase (fee loss)

### Expiry
Expiration date of the capital loss, after which it can no longer be offset

### Tax Carryforward (Zainetto Fiscale)
Tracks total capital losses yet to be offset, and handles removing them upon expiration

### Fees to Recover
ETF purchase fees not yet added to the Tax Carryforward. They are added by the next sale of the ETF at a gain, and dropped if every unit is sold before that

### Taxable Cap Gain
Capital gain on which tax will actually be paid. Reduced by the Tax Carryforward in case of offsettable capital losses.

*Example:* with a gain of 20€ and 5€ in the Tax Carryforward, tax is paid only on 20 − 5 = 15€, instead of 20€

### Tax Bracket
Tax bracket of the asset (e.g., 26% for Stocks)

### Tax
Tax actually paid, calculated on the taxable capital gain

### P&L
Profit & Loss of the sale. Calculated as *Gross Cap Gain − Tax*

### Current Cash
Cash available in the account after the operation

### Asset Value
Value of securities held in the account after the operation

### NAV
Net Asset Value after the operation. Calculated as *Current Cash + Asset Value*

### Committed Cash
Total liquidity injected into the account. Considers only deposits and withdrawals
